House of the Count Party

CLIENT

ROLE

Illustration / Visual Direction

YEAR

2026

CATEGORY

KV / Illustration

ABOUT

Sansiri wanted a Halloween event poster for its residents — something cute, illustrated, and still a little spooky.

I handled the illustration and set the visual direction for the project. The main challenge was finding the balance between playful and spooky, without making the event feel too dark or too serious.

This ended up being one of the projects I had the most fun working on.

PROCESS

Somehow, the first draft worked.

I developed the illustration and visual direction quite freely, which made the whole process genuinely fun to work on.

And, oh my god, there were almost no revisions. The client was already happy with the first draft — only a few small text adjustments were needed.

A rare and beautiful moment.
