ABOUT

An illustrated key visual created for King Power during Songkran in 2022.

The project has been sitting in my archive for quite a while, so I honestly don't remember every detail of the brief anymore. What I do remember is enjoying the slightly Thai character of the visual.

It still feels playful and contemporary, while carrying just enough of that Songkran and Thai atmosphere for me to still like looking at it years later.

LOOKING BACK

I don't remember everything. I still like the work though.

This one is old enough that most of the process has disappeared from my memory.

But I still remember liking the balance between illustration, Songkran, and a little bit of Thai visual flavor.

Maybe that's enough.
